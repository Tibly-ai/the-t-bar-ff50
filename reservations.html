RESERVE A TASTING

Step away from the rush of daily life and enter a space of quiet ritual. Join our tea master for an intimate, guided journey through rare, wild-harvested Japanese green teas, seasonal ceremonial matcha, and aged leaves, served in hand-thrown wood-fired vessels.
