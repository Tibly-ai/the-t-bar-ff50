Our Origin

Sourced from Living Earth.

At The T Bar, our journey began in the mist-shrouded hills of Shizuoka and the clay pits of Tokoname. We set out to find growers who prioritize the long-term health of the soil over commercial yield.

Every leaf in our collection is wild-harvested or grown using sustainable, generational farming techniques that respect the natural environment. We trade directly with independent families, ensuring fair compensation that preserves their heritage.

The Soil & The Leaf

Our volcanic soils in Kyushu and Shizuoka receive no synthetic inputs, allowing the natural character of the leaf to express itself fully.

The Journey

Rooted in Respect, Harvested with Patience

Our commitment to sustainable, fair-trade tea sourcing is sustained by four core pillars that guide our partnerships across East Asia.

01

Direct Sourcing

We bypass industrial markets to work directly with generational growers. This ensures full transparency and fair-trade pricing that flows directly back to the farms.

Kyoto & Shizuoka
02

Living Clay

Our ceramic teaware is hand-thrown using raw, untreated clay. We partner with individual potters who respect the mineral heritage of Tokoname and Shigaraki.

Tokoname & Shigaraki
03

Wild-Harvesting

Several of our signature tea selections are harvested from semi-wild plants, growing naturally without intervention among native forest flora.

Kyushu Highlands
04

Slow Crafting

From picking to shade-growing and traditional wood-firing, every stage of our production values the slow, meditative process over speed.
