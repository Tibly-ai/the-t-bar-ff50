The Slow Method

How we brew your cup

I

Water Tempering

We heat local volcanic spring water to precise temperatures between seventy and eighty degrees, protecting delicate amino acids.

II

Clay Seasoning

Each batch is steeped inside unglazed porous purple clay pots which absorb harsh minerals and round out the brew.

III

Timed Steeps

We time standard infusions down to the exact second, drawing out sweet umami tones without drawing bitter tannins.

IV

Tactile Service

We present your tea in custom hand-thrown stoneware cups, grounding the tasting through the organic texture of raw earth.
